Principal Investigator

Annie Nigra, ScM, PhD

Annie Nigra, ScM, PhD, is an environmental epidemiologist and Assistant Professor in the Department of Epidemiology in the Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth College. In addition to leading the WATER EPI Lab, Annie is the Director of the EARTH Program and is engaged with several large research centers.
